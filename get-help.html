Urgent after childbirth

Postpartum psychosis can be a medical emergency.

Sudden severe confusion, hallucinations, delusions, extreme agitation or unusually elevated behaviour with little or no sleep after birth need urgent in-person medical assessment. Do not wait for a routine appointment. Call 112 or go to an emergency department now.

If you are supporting someone

  • Stay with them if it is safe. Listen without arguing or trying to solve everything at once.
  • Ask what would help them feel safer in the next few minutes.
  • For immediate danger, severe confusion or a medical emergency, call 112 or go to an emergency department.
  • Do not promise confidentiality if someone's immediate safety is at risk. Get human support.
