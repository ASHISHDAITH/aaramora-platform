A future, carefully moderated space

Connection should come with care.

A peer community is a future product area, not a live feature in this prototype. It should be built only after moderation, safeguarding, escalation and privacy operations are ready.

Possible support circles

  • Overthinking and racing thoughts
  • Anxiety and panic experiences
  • Loneliness, grief and relationship strain
  • Burnout and emotional exhaustion
  • Women's health and mental wellbeing
  • Small everyday wins and practical support

Safety before launch

  • Pseudonyms and no requirement to disclose personal history
  • Trained human moderation, report and block tools
  • Content warnings and limits on graphic material
  • No diagnosis, coercion, harassment or dangerous advice
  • No popularity ranking, engagement feed or follower counts
  • Clear crisis routes and a documented escalation policy

Not live yet: There is no public posting, direct messaging, user account, peer matching or live listener service in this prototype. Stories must be consented, moderated and clearly distinguished from clinical guidance.
