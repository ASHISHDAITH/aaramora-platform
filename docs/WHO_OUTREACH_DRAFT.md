# Draft enquiry to WHO

**Status:** Draft only · not sent  
**Suggested route:** WHO South-East Asia / WHO India public enquiry or the official WHO contact route. Confirm the right recipient before sending. Do not invent an email address.

## Subject

Enquiry about Hindi and Marathi resources for *Doing What Matters in Times of Stress*

## Message

Dear WHO Mental Health and Substance Use team,

I am developing AARAMORA, an India-focused, multilingual mental-wellbeing information and help-navigation prototype. We are reviewing WHO's *Doing What Matters in Times of Stress: An Illustrated Guide* as a source for readers looking for evidence-informed ways to cope with stress.

The WHO publication page currently lists a Hindi edition. In the language and audio listings we reviewed on 24 November 2026, we could not find a Marathi edition or Hindi/Marathi audio exercises.

Could you please advise:

1. Whether a Marathi edition or Marathi audio version is planned, or whether an existing official version is available through another WHO or WHO South-East Asia resource.
2. Whether WHO has a recommended India-based translation, linguistic review or dissemination partner for Marathi.
3. Whether any country-specific notes, translator guidance or updated attribution requirements should accompany a non-commercial Hindi/Marathi adaptation.
4. Whether WHO welcomes collaboration or notification when an independent organisation creates a non-commercial adaptation under the publication's stated CC BY-NC-SA licence.

We understand that WHO's published copyright policy allows non-commercial translations and adaptations of eligible CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO materials under the same or similar licence terms, with attribution and the prescribed disclaimer. We will not use the WHO logo, imply WHO endorsement or reproduce third-party visuals without separate rights review.

Our intention is to keep the resource accessible, culturally appropriate and clearly distinguished from clinical treatment. Any Hindi or Marathi wording/audio would undergo independent native-language, clinical and safeguarding review before publication.

Thank you for your guidance.

Kind regards,  
AARAMORA project team
