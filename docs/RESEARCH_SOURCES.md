# Design Research Sources and Evidence Notes

Research checked: 30 October 2026. These references inform design choices; they do not prove that a particular interface, colour, mascot or story treats trauma.

## Trauma-informed practice
- SAMHSA, *Trauma-Informed Approaches and Programs*: https://www.samhsa.gov/mental-health/trauma-violence/trauma-informed-approaches-programs
  - Principles include safety, trustworthiness and transparency, peer support, collaboration, empowerment/voice/choice and cultural/historical/gender considerations.
- UK Government, *Working definition of trauma-informed practice*: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/working-definition-of-trauma-informed-practice/working-definition-of-trauma-informed-practice
  - Summarises safety, trust, choice, collaboration, empowerment and cultural consideration.
- Eggleston et al. (2025), *A scoping review of trauma-informed care principles applied in design and technology*: https://doi.org/10.1177/20552076251360925
  - Screened 118 studies; found trauma-informed principles were not consistently or methodically applied in digital design. This supports explicit design and governance requirements, not a claim that a visual style is therapeutic.

## Psychological first aid and human support
- WHO, *Psychological first aid: Guide for field workers*: https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241548205
  - Describes humane, supportive, practical assistance that respects dignity, culture and abilities.
- WHO PFA pocket guide: https://iris.who.int/bitstream/handle/10665/102380/9789241548618_eng.pdf
  - Look, Listen, Link: attend to safety and urgent needs, listen without pressure, connect people with practical information and services.

## Mental-health app quality and co-production
- Mehrotra et al. (2025), *Evaluating Characteristics and Quality of Mental Health Apps Available in App Stores for Indian Users*: https://doi.org/10.2196/79238
  - In 350 reviewed apps accessible in India, 65.1% did not report mental-health professional involvement; 10.9% referenced empirical research; 15.4% did not mention a data-sharing policy; 30% attempted to dispel myths. There is an erratum dated 30 March 2026; consult the updated publication before quoting the paper.
- Brotherdale et al. (2024), *Co-producing digital mental health interventions: A systematic review*: https://doi.org/10.1177/20552076241239172
  - 26 studies across 24 interventions; end users were rarely involved throughout all design, development and evaluation stages. Co-production can improve cultural fit and acceptability, but power imbalances and inconsistent roles remain concerns.
- Valentine et al. (2025), *A meta-analysis of persuasive design, engagement, and efficacy in 92 RCTs of mental health apps*: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41746-025-01567-5
  - Across 92 RCTs and 16,728 participants, the analysis found no significant association between persuasive design principles and engagement or efficacy. Do not add streaks or guilt-based retention mechanics.

## Accessibility and content
- W3C, *Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.2*: https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/
- GOV.UK Design Principles: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/government-design-principles
  - Start with user needs, understand context, build useful services rather than pages for their own sake.
- NHS Digital Service Manual, *Standard for creating health content*: https://service-manual.nhs.uk/content/standard-for-creating-health-content
  - Plain language, inclusive content, user consultation, WCAG 2.2 AA.
- GOV.UK Design System accessibility strategy: https://design-system.service.gov.uk/accessibility/accessibility-strategy/

## Source interpretation
- Research findings justify safety, transparency, co-design, accessibility, privacy and restraint.
- Creative hypotheses include the paper/sage/forest palette, Kiran firefly, garden-path metaphor and fictional story.
- Those hypotheses must be tested with paid lived-experience participants, native Hindi/Marathi speakers, clinicians and accessibility testers.
- Do not copy copyrighted characters, artwork, wording, page layouts or books. Use sources to inform original work.
