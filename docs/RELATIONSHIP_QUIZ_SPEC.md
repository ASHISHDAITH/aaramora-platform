# Relationship Reflection Quiz Specification

Three original optional self-reflections and one non-scored safety check appear on `relationships.html`. They are educational prompts, not validated psychometric instruments, clinical screens or diagnoses.

- **Attachment protest reflection:** repeated messages, checking, interpreting delays as rejection, indirect tests/withdrawal, high-intensity protest words and difficulty focusing while waiting.
- **Conflict pattern reflection:** pursuing, withdrawing and repair/pause behaviours.
- **Boundaries and mutual respect:** expressing limits, accepting another person's space, consent/privacy, autonomy and ability to disagree safely.
- **Safety check:** fear, monitoring/control/isolation, sexual pressure, threats/stalking or physical harm.

No numeric score, cutoff, attachment identity, partner rating, compatibility result, risk level or stay/leave recommendation is generated. Answers are optional and held in page memory only. Unanswered safety questions are not treated as No. A Yes or Not sure response prioritises private support, not couple-repair exercises. Clinician and safeguarding review is required before launch.
