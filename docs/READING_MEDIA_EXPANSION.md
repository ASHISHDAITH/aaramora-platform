# AARAMORA Reading Library: media-source expansion

**Research snapshot:** 24 November 2026

This document records the 22 source discoveries added to the reading catalogue, the media formats assigned to each and the permissions that must be respected. Existing reading shelves and the original 26 multilingual entries remain. The website stores discovery metadata and source links; it does not store the full text, audio or video from these external sources.

## Browse formats

Each source record is tagged with one or more of the following:
- **Book:** open textbooks, scriptures, public-domain editions, source text catalogues and lawful library discovery.
- **Video:** creator-published explainers or media discovery pages.
- **Audiobook:** public-domain readings, scripture recitations and official audio readers.
- **Article:** research articles, workbooks, educational information and source articles.
- **Blog:** publisher essays and recurring editorial learning.
- **Image:** individually licensed photographs, archival images and openly licensed image discovery.

A format tag identifies a type of source. It does not imply that AARAMORA hosts, owns or may redistribute that source material.

## Research and rights boundaries

1. Free to read, free to download, public domain, open access, and permission to redistribute are different states.
2. Copyright is territory-specific. An edition listed as public domain in the United States may not be public domain in India or elsewhere.
3. The licence of a platform or repository does not automatically cover every bundled book, translation, photograph, recording, contributor or piece of artwork.
4. YouTube, audio services, scholarly repositories, scripture readers and stock-media platforms should be accessed by their official page, supported API or embed tools, subject to their terms. No DRM, paywall or access-control bypass is used.
5. The catalogue stores metadata and links only. Full copyrighted books, article bodies, creator videos and protected recordings are not copied into the repository.
6. The existing OpenStax Psychology 2e entry remains separate with its CC BY-NC-SA terms and AI-ingestion restriction. The newly catalogued OpenStax Psychology edition is the one whose own preface names CC BY 4.0.
7. No entry's editorial-review status was changed to “reviewed”. Native-language fidelity, religious/cultural context and manual assistive-technology checks remain pending until named qualified reviewers complete them.

## Maintenance checklist

Before adding or changing a resource, record its title, creator/author, edition/version, canonical URL, format, access type, item-level rights statement, jurisdiction caveat, attribution, accessibility notes, source-check date and next-review date. Do not label an item as downloadable or reusable solely because it opens without a payment prompt.


## Format and rights corrections · 24 November 2026

The catalogue now distinguishes **Audio** from **Audiobook**. Audio is used for podcasts, recitations, guided practices and other source audio. Audiobook is reserved for a narrated book/audio-book edition. This is reflected in both the data tags and user-facing filters.

The new Project Gutenberg audio-book record points to the publisher's official Audio Books collection at https://www.gutenberg.org/. The publisher describes human-read titles and the Open Audiobook Collection, and states that its digitizations are free and public domain in the United States. Check each source title, narrator/recording and local copyright position separately.

The identified OpenStax Psychology edition is CC BY 4.0 with attribution, but OpenStax separately states that it must not be used to train or ingest into LLMs or generative-AI offerings without prior written permission. The CC BY licence does not erase that explicit restriction. This source-specific note is stored on the record.
