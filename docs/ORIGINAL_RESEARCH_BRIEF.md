The website shouldn't feel like a hospital, medical portal, social media feed, or productivity app.

It should feel like a **safe place you can enter when your mind feels too loud.**

People might arrive thinking:

- "I can't stop thinking."
- "I'm tired of pretending I'm okay."
- "I feel empty and don't know why."
- "My trauma keeps coming back."
- "I feel alone."
- "I can't explain what I'm feeling."
- "I need help, but I'm not ready to talk to anyone."
- "I don't know what to do with my life."

The website should help them take one small step without demanding too much.

# **8. How the community should work**

- Overthinking & racing thoughts
- Anxiety & panic
- Trauma recovery support
- Loneliness
- Grief

- Relationship pain
- Self-esteem
- Daily small victories
- "I just need someone to listen"

### **Safety features**

- Anonymous or pseudonymous profiles
- No requirement to reveal personal details
- Content warnings
- No graphic trauma descriptions in public feeds
- Report and block features
- Active moderation
- Professional review of safety policies
- Rules against diagnosis, abuse, coercion, and dangerous advice
- Crisis resources visible when necessary
- No follower counts, popularity contests, or engagement rankings
- No algorithm that pushes distressing content for engagement

A user should be able to choose **"I don't want advice, I just want to be heard."**

# **9. How we should make money without exploiting people**

Mental health users should never feel that their pain is being monetized.

Good options:

- Free core support resources
- Optional premium creative experiences
- Affordable guided courses reviewed by professionals
- Donations
- Grants from mental health organizations
- University and NGO partnerships
- Ethical sponsorships
- Corporate wellbeing licensing, with no access to individual user data
- Optional therapist directory referrals with transparent policies

Avoid:

- Selling personal emotional data
- Ads targeting people based on mental health conditions
- Making people pay to access crisis information
- Streak loss or guilt-based notifications
- Claiming the site can cure trauma or mental illness
- AI pretending to be a therapist

## **Project concept: "A Place Within"**

Working title only. The brand name can be researched later.

A digital sanctuary built around the idea that people don't always need an answer immediately. Sometimes they need a quiet moment, a tool, a story, a connection, or a path to professional help.

The central experience:

**Arrive → Feel → Choose → Experience → Reflect → Connect**

- **Arrive:** No account, no pressure.
- **Feel:** Choose what's happening inside.
- **Choose:** Select the kind of help you want.
- **Experience:** A game, grounding exercise, story, journal, audio, or guide.
- **Reflect:** Optional thoughts about how you feel.
- **Connect:** Community or professional support if desired.

The website should never imply that someone needs to complete a journey to be healed. They can leave, return, repeat, skip, or do nothing.
