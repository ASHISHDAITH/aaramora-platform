What this site does, and what it does not.

PROTOTYPE · REVIEW STATUS SHOWN

Privacy and network behaviour

  • No visitor accounts, analytics, advertising pixels or third-party tracking scripts are installed.
  • Site pages, styles, illustrations and the brand film are served from AARAMORA’s Vercel deployment. No external image or video CDN is loaded automatically.
  • External providers and map sites are contacted only after you choose a link. Their own privacy policies then apply.
  • Tools run in your browser. AARAMORA does not upload or persist the words you enter. Printing and copying are controlled by your browser/device.
  • The offline support page is cached after you first open the site online. Static pages/assets are cached, not entered form content.
  • Vercel and network providers may record technical request logs. AARAMORA does not control provider-side logging or retention.
  • Quick Exit replaces this tab with a blank page. It does not erase browser history, downloads, network logs or shared-device activity.

Review status

  • Women's-health material: draft, awaiting qualified women's-health clinician review.
  • Research spotlight: draft, awaiting independent research/editorial review.
  • Hindi and Marathi support labels: draft translations awaiting native-speaker review.
  • Religious and cultural interpretation: no independent sign-off is recorded.
  • Accessibility: automated checks do not replace keyboard, screen-reader, zoom and human testing.

Safety boundaries

  • AARAMORA is not a therapist, doctor, telemedicine service or emergency dispatch provider.
  • No diagnosis, prescription, treatment promise, risk score or AI chatbot is provided.
  • Activities are optional. There are no scores, streaks or leaderboards.
  • No public peer community, direct messages or live listener service is active.
  • Only Tele-MANAS 14416 and Emergency 112 are shown as national support numbers until named human review is completed for any additional number.
