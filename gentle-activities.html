Activities · optional · no score

Choose something small. Or skip everything.

These are optional prompts, not therapy or treatment. Stop if anything feels uncomfortable, and skip anything that does not suit you.

Orient to the room

Let your eyes rest on three ordinary objects. Notice one colour, one shape and one sound. If this feels uncomfortable, skip it.

Put one thought into words

Write one sentence about what is taking up space in your mind. You can keep it private or close the page without saving.

Choose one small next step

Pick something that can begin in a few minutes: drink water, open a window, message someone you trust or rest. It is okay if today is not a task day.

Notice what supports you

Feel the chair, bed or floor beneath you. You do not need to change your breathing or hold a position. Move or stop whenever you choose.
