World Health Organization · Illustrated guide · 2020

Before a book is mirrored

Our reuse checklist

  • A repository being public does not place every bundled book in the public domain.
  • Record title, authors, edition, source URL, access date and exact licence version.
  • Check that the licence applies to the book text and file, not only the website or GitHub code.
  • Check images, charts, worksheets, cover art and third-party notices separately.
  • Show attribution, licence link and changes. Respect NonCommercial and ShareAlike conditions.
  • Never bypass DRM, paywalls, private repository permissions or download restrictions.
  • Do not upload unofficial scans. For copyrighted titles, link to the publisher or lawful library.
