HINDU TEXTS · SOURCE READING + SELECT PUBLIC-DOMAIN EDITIONS

Bhagavad Gita, Upanishads and classical Hindu texts

Explore the Bhagavad Gita, principal Upanishads, Valmiki Ramayana, Ramcharitmanas, Yoga Sutras, Brahma Sutra and selected Sanskrit commentarial traditions through the Heritage IIT Kanpur Gita Supersite and its linked collections.

For a historical English rendering, Project Gutenberg hosts Sir Edwin Arnold’s The Song Celestial; Or, Bhagavad-Gîtâ (1885). Its catalogue describes the ebook as public domain in the United States; readers elsewhere should check local copyright law before reusing or redistributing it.

The age of a scripture does not automatically place every translation, commentary, typesetting or digital edition in the public domain. Read through the source institution and check rights for the exact edition before copying it.

ISLAM · VERIFIED ARABIC TEXT + TRANSLATIONS

The Quran and translated meanings

Tanzil provides a verified Quran text for reading and download. Its text licence permits verbatim copying and distribution under CC BY 3.0, but the Quran text must not be altered and copies must retain the required source and licence notice.

QuranEnc provides translations with separate republication conditions. Its terms require the text to remain unchanged, source and version details to be preserved, and other stated conditions to be followed.

Treat each translation as a separate work. Do not merge, edit or relabel translations, and do not assume a permission for the Arabic text applies to a translated edition.

OPEN-ACCESS BOOKS · METADATA + LIBRARY LENDING

Research books and lawful digital borrowing

Use the Directory of Open Access Books (DOAB) to find scholarly books with publisher-stated open-access information. Use Open Library to discover editions and check whether a title is available to borrow through its lending controls.

DOAB’s CC0 statement applies to its metadata, not automatically to the books listed there. Open Library borrowing depends on the title’s availability and lending terms; a catalogue record is not a free-download permission. Use official APIs and access rules rather than scraping catalogue pages in bulk.

Before a book is mirrored

Our reuse checklist

  • A repository being public does not place every bundled book in the public domain.
  • Record title, authors, edition, source URL, access date and exact licence version.
  • Check that the licence applies to the book text and file, not only the website or GitHub code.
  • Check images, charts, worksheets, cover art and third-party notices separately.
  • Show attribution, licence link and changes. Respect NonCommercial and ShareAlike conditions.
  • Never bypass DRM, paywalls, private repository permissions or download restrictions.
  • Do not upload unofficial scans. For copyrighted titles, link to the publisher or lawful library.
