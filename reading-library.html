Before a book is mirrored

Our reuse checklist

  • A public repository or free-to-read page does not make every book or translation public domain.
  • Check the copyright status of the work, edition, translator, commentary and illustrations separately, and check the law in the reader's country.
  • Religious texts can have public-domain source-language editions alongside copyrighted modern translations and commentaries.
  • Record title, authors, edition, source URL, access date and exact licence version.
  • Check that the licence applies to the book text and file, not only the website or GitHub code.
  • Check images, charts, worksheets, cover art and third-party notices separately.
  • Show attribution, licence link and changes. Respect NonCommercial and ShareAlike conditions.
  • Never bypass DRM, paywalls, private repository permissions, borrowing limits or download restrictions.
  • Respect source-specific no-scraping and no-AI-training notices. AARAMORA's catalogue links to those sites without copying their text.
  • Do not upload unofficial scans. For copyrighted titles, link to the publisher or lawful library.
