Words Library · copy what helps

Small ways to say something difficult.

Use these as starting points and adapt them to your own voice.

Ask for space

I want to talk about this, but I feel overwhelmed. Can we pause and agree on a time to return to it?

Tell someone I am not okay

I am having a difficult time. I do not need you to fix it. Could you stay with me or check in again later?

Ask for practical help

Could you help with one practical thing today, such as food, travel or making an appointment?

Set a boundary

I am not comfortable with that. I can discuss another option, but I need this boundary to be respected.

Prepare for a professional visit

I find it difficult to explain everything in the room. I have written a short summary. Could we start with that?

Support someone else

Thank you for telling me. Would you like me to listen, help with one practical step or sit with you while you contact support?
