Quick Exit

Privacy summary

Your information stays with you in these tools.

AARAMORA is a static help-navigation prototype. It has no visitor accounts, no server-side forms and no behavioural analytics.

What the site does

  • Serves its pages, illustrations, styles and versioned film files from the AARAMORA Vercel deployment.
  • Does not automatically load Unsplash, jsDelivr video or third-party fonts on the homepage.
  • Runs the Safety Plan and Appointment Bridge in the browser, with no form submission or persistence to AARAMORA servers.
  • Caches selected static support files on this device after the first online visit so the support page can open offline.
  • May be subject to technical request logs held by Vercel and network providers.

What happens when you follow an external link

When you choose a provider, government or map link, your browser contacts that third party. Their privacy terms and logs then apply.

Quick Exit limitations

Quick Exit replaces the current tab with a blank page. It does not clear browser history, device downloads, cached pages, network logs or activity on a shared device.
