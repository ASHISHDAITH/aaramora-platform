When this worksheet is not the right first step

If you are in immediate danger, may hurt yourself or someone else, or need urgent medical help, step away from this worksheet and contact a person or emergency support. In India call 112. Tele-MANAS at 14416 offers mental-health support. If someone is monitoring your device or you feel unsafe with another person, use a safer device or private support route when possible.

Women's health · appointment preparation

Your concerns deserve time and context.

Use this optional planner to organise what you may want to tell a clinician. It does not identify PMOS, endometriosis, PMDD, menopause or another condition.

Which areas have you noticed? Choose only what you want to share.

This page uses temporary in-memory form values only. It does not save, upload or analyse your answers. Do not use a device you believe is monitored if that may put you at risk.

Questions you can take to the appointment

  • What else might explain these symptoms?
  • Which examination or tests are useful, and why?
  • What are the benefits, risks and alternatives for each option?
  • How could this affect sleep, mood, fertility or other health concerns?
  • When should I seek urgent help or return for review?

You can ask for an explanation in a language you are comfortable using. You do not need to answer every question on this website before seeking care.

For symptoms that may follow a cycle

A paper log can show timing better than a one-time quiz.

For possible PMDD or recurring premenstrual symptoms, a clinician may ask for prospective daily notes across at least two consecutive menstrual cycles. This blank log is not the DRSP scale and cannot diagnose PMDD.

Print the blank pages and use them on paper if useful. Record what you notice in your own words: mood, physical symptoms, cycle timing and impact on daily life. Skip any column that does not fit. Do not use a score or threshold from this worksheet.

Urgent symptoms should not wait for a diary. Seek urgent medical advice for severe or sudden pelvic/abdominal pain, fainting, very heavy bleeding with weakness/dizziness, or other worrying physical symptoms. In an immediate emergency in India, call 112.
Why Aaramora does not diagnose through quizzes

Clinical screeners need a care pathway behind them.

Recognised tools can support clinical practice, but a questionnaire alone does not diagnose a condition or decide what care is right.

Depression and anxiety

PHQ-9 and GAD-7 are screening instruments. Positive responses need suitable assessment and follow-up. Aaramora links to official resources rather than scoring them in this static prototype.

Official PHQ/GAD resources →

Pregnancy and postpartum

EPDS and PHQ-9 may be used in perinatal care. Screening needs a named clinical response for positive answers and urgent concerns. Postpartum psychosis requires emergency assessment.
