# A Place Within
## Global mental-health support website: benchmark & product blueprint

### Purpose
Create a gentle digital sanctuary for people experiencing overthinking, anxiety, stress, emotional pain, trauma-related distress, loneliness, burnout, and related concerns. It should offer an approachable first step, evidence-informed self-help, creativity, connection, and clear routes to professional help—without claiming to replace treatment.

### North-star experience
**Arrive → Feel → Choose → Experience → Reflect → Connect**
- Arrive: browse without signup; no pressure to disclose.
- Feel: choose a current feeling or need.
- Choose: calm, learn, create, play gently, read, connect, or seek care.
- Experience: brief accessible activity, audio, guide, or story.
- Reflect: optional private note or check-in.
- Connect: community or professional resources if wanted.

### How to rank during proper research
Score each site 1–5 across:
- Evidence & clinical credibility (20%)
- User experience and visual design (15%)
- Accessibility & inclusion (15%)
- Safety, privacy, moderation (15%)
- Creative engagement (10%)
- Help-seeking / professional pathways (10%)
- Community quality (5%)
- Global reach, language, affordability (10%)
Scores should be evidence-based, link to reviewed sources, and dated. Treat ranking as editorial judgement rather than absolute truth. Verify current service status, official URLs, screenshots, availability, languages, and privacy policies. Don't infer a website's current feature set from this candidate list.

### Product differentiation: combine what is usually split across products
- **Healing Garden:** a peaceful virtual garden responds to optional activities (no pressure, streaks, scores, or punitive progress).
- **Safe Corner:** custom comfort space with visual ambience, grounding objects, audio, writing, and preferred activities.
- **Overthinking Unload:** private thought capture with optional sorting: facts, predictions, fears, controllables, and one tiny next step. Avoid presenting this as diagnosis.
- **Let-It-Go River:** write a thought on a leaf/boat and let it drift
