Urgent after childbirth: postpartum psychosis can be a medical emergency.

Sudden confusion, hallucinations, delusions, severe agitation or unusually high/manic behaviour after birth need urgent in-person medical assessment. Do not wait for a routine appointment. In an immediate emergency in India, call 112.
